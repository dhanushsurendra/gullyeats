Terms of use

What GullyEats does

GullyEats is a tool that independent street food vendors use to take orders. The food, its price, its preparation and the service come from the vendor, not from GullyEats. We don't cook, deliver or sell food.

Placing an order

  • You don't need an account. Pick items, place the order and you get a token number.
  • Prices are set by the vendor. The total includes a platform fee of ₹2, shown before you order.
  • An order is a request to the vendor. The vendor can decline or cancel it, for example if an item runs out.
  • Your place in the queue is an estimate, counted in the order customers paid. The vendor may prepare orders in a different order.

Paying

You pay the vendor directly at the cart, in cash or by UPI. GullyEats does not take or hold your money. Cooking starts after you pay.

Changing or cancelling

  • Until you pay, you can add or change items, or cancel, from your order page.
  • After you pay, ask the vendor to change or cancel your order. Any refund is given back to you by the vendor at the cart.

Food, allergies and complaints

The vendor is responsible for the food, its hygiene and its ingredients. If you have an allergy or dietary need, ask the vendor before ordering. Take problems with food or service to the vendor first. If that doesn't solve it, contact us and we'll try to help.

Fair use

Please don't place fake orders, try to break or overload the service, or use it for anything unlawful. We may block access that harms vendors or other customers.

Our responsibility

We work to keep GullyEats running, but it may sometimes be slow or unavailable. To the extent the law allows, GullyEats is not responsible for the food or for what happens between you and the vendor. Nothing here limits your rights under Indian consumer protection law.

Changes and law

We may update these terms; the date at the top shows the latest version. They are governed by the laws of India, and the courts in Bengaluru, Karnataka have jurisdiction.

Privacy

We collect only what your order needs. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

What we collect

Your order

Items, prices, token number, times and its status.

To make your order and keep you updated on it.

Your mobile number (optional)

Only if you type it in when ordering.

So the vendor can reach you about that order. Never for marketing.

Rating and feedback (optional)

The stars and words you leave after your food is served.

To help the vendor and GullyEats improve.

On your phone

Your basket, the number you typed and a short list of your recent orders, in your browser's storage.

So your order is still there if you go back or close the tab. Clearing your browser data removes it.

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, and we never ask for card or UPI details.

Who sees it

  • The vendor you order from sees your order, and your number if you gave one, only to serve you.
  • Other customers at the same cart see only token numbers in the queue, never your items or number.
  • Companies that run GullyEats for us: MongoDB Atlas (database), Render and Vercel (hosting). They handle data only to provide that service.
  • Authorities, when Indian law requires it.

How long we keep it

Orders are kept for the vendor's records and as long as tax and accounting laws require. Your number is kept only with the order you gave it for. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask what personal data we hold about you, and have it corrected or erased. You can also nominate someone to act for you. Write to our Grievance Officer below. We reply within 90 days at the latest. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Security

Your data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and only the vendor you ordered from and GullyEats can see your order details. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you as the law requires.

Children

Anyone can order food, but children should not enter a mobile number without a parent's permission.
