Terms & Privacy

GullyEats lets you order from a street food cart by scanning its QR code, and gives the cart an app to run its orders. These terms apply when you use the GullyEats ordering website, the vendor app or the onboarding app. The privacy policy explains what we collect and why.

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Terms for customers

What GullyEats does

GullyEats is a tool that independent street food vendors use to take orders. The food, its price, its preparation and the service come from the vendor, not from GullyEats. We don't cook, deliver or sell food.

Placing an order

  • You don't need an account. You pick items, place the order and get a token number.
  • Prices are set by the vendor. The total includes a platform fee of ₹2, shown before you order.
  • An order is a request to the vendor. The vendor can decline or cancel it, for example if an item runs out.
  • Your place in the queue is an estimate, counted in the order customers paid. The vendor may prepare orders in a different order.

Paying

You pay the vendor directly at the cart, in cash or by UPI. GullyEats does not take or hold your money. Cooking starts after you pay.

Changing or cancelling

  • You can add or change items, or cancel, from your order page while you haven't paid.
  • Once you have paid, ask the vendor to change or cancel your order. Any refund is given back to you by the vendor at the cart.

Food, allergies and complaints

The vendor is responsible for the food, its hygiene, its ingredients and any food safety licence it needs. If you have an allergy or dietary need, ask the vendor before ordering. Problems with food or service should first go to the vendor. If that doesn't solve it, contact us (see Contact) and we'll try to help.

Fair use

Please don't place fake orders, try to break or overload the service, or use it for anything unlawful. We may block access that harms vendors or other customers.

Terms for vendors and staff

Your account

  • A GullyEats team member sets up your cart with your mobile number. You log in with your Vendor ID or phone number and a 4-digit PIN.
  • Keep your PIN secret. You are responsible for what happens under your account and under the staff logins you create.
  • Staff accounts can take orders, mark them paid, preparing or served, change stock and open or close the cart. Only the owner can change the menu, the cart details, staff logins, or see sales and fee bills.

Your menu and food

  • You are responsible for your menu, prices, photos and the food you serve, including food safety (FSSAI) registration and any local permits your cart needs.
  • Only upload photos you have the right to use. They are shown publicly on your menu.
  • Menu item names are shown in Hindi, Kannada and Tamil by sound (for example "Masala Dosa" written in that script). These are made automatically and may be wrong. You can correct them in the app.

Orders and customer payments

Customers pay you directly. Mark an order paid only when you have received the money, and give back any refund the app shows. Use customer phone numbers only to contact that customer about their order, never for marketing.

Platform fee

  • GullyEats charges ₹2 for each order a customer pays for, counted by the day (India time) it was paid.
  • Each day's fee can be paid in the app through Razorpay after that day ends. If it is still unpaid 26 hours after the day began, the cart stops taking new orders until it is paid.
  • Fees for orders cancelled after a day was billed are credited on a later bill. Paid fees are otherwise not refundable, except where a payment was taken twice, which is refunded automatically.

Suspension

We may pause a cart or disable an account for unpaid fees, misuse, fake orders, avoiding the platform fee, or complaints of serious harm. Where we can, we'll tell you why and how to fix it.

For everyone

Availability

We work to keep GullyEats running, but it may sometimes be slow or unavailable, for example when your phone or the network is offline. We are not responsible for losses caused by an outage beyond our reasonable control.

Limit of responsibility

To the extent the law allows, GullyEats is not responsible for the food, the transactions between customers and vendors, or indirect losses. Nothing here limits rights you have under Indian consumer protection law.

Privacy policy

We collect only what the service needs, and we don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

What we collect

WhoWhatWhy
Customers Your order (items, prices, token number, times). Your mobile number, only if you choose to give it. Your star rating and feedback, if you leave them. To make and track your order, let the vendor reach you about it, and help vendors improve.
Vendors Name, mobile number, Vendor ID, cart name, city, address and map location, cart and menu photos, menu and prices, orders and sales, platform fee payments. To run your account and cart, show your menu to customers, and bill and collect the platform fee.
Staff Name, mobile number, staff ID, the cart you work at. To let you log in and take orders.

PINs and one-time passwords are stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form that can't be read back. We never see or store card or UPI details: platform fee payments are handled by Razorpay, and we keep only the payment reference.

On your phone

The ordering website keeps your basket, the mobile number you entered and a short list of your recent orders in your browser's storage, so your order is still there if you go back or close the tab. Clearing your browser data removes it. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Who we share it with

  • The vendor you order from sees your order, and your mobile number if you gave one, only to serve you.
  • Other customers waiting at the same cart see only token numbers in the queue, never your items or number.
  • Service providers that run GullyEats for us: Razorpay (fee payments), Amazon Web Services (photo storage), MongoDB Atlas (database), Render and Vercel (hosting), and Google Input Tools (menu item names only, to write them in Hindi, Kannada and Tamil). They process data only to provide their service.
  • Authorities, when Indian law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep order and account data while the vendor's account is active and as long as tax, accounting or other laws require. Customer mobile numbers are kept only with the order they were given for. You can ask us to delete your data sooner (see below).

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to tell you what personal data we hold about you, correct it, or erase it, and you can withdraw consent you gave. You can also nominate someone to act for you. Write to us at the address below. We will reply as soon as we can, and within 90 days. If you're not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS), access to vendor data needs a login, and each cart's data is visible only to its owner, its staff and GullyEats. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Children

Vendor and staff accounts are for adults. Anyone can order food, but we don't knowingly collect personal data from children. A child should not enter a mobile number without a parent's permission.

Contact and grievances

For questions, complaints, or requests about your data, write to our Grievance Officer at support@gullyeats.com. Please include your cart name or order token number if it's about an order. We acknowledge complaints within 48 hours and aim to resolve them within 30 days.
