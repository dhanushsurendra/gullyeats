Terms and conditions

What GullyEats provides

GullyEats gives your cart a QR code menu that customers order from, and an app to run those orders. You sell the food; GullyEats provides the ordering tool.

Your account

  • A GullyEats team member sets up your cart with your mobile number. You log in with your Vendor ID or phone number and a 4-digit PIN.
  • Keep your PIN secret. You are responsible for what happens under your account and under the staff logins you create.
  • Staff can take orders, mark them paid, preparing or served, change stock and open or close the cart. Only the owner can change the menu, the cart details and staff logins, and see sales and fee bills.

Your menu and food

  • You are responsible for your menu, prices, photos and the food you serve, including food safety (FSSAI) registration and any local permits your cart needs.
  • Only upload photos you have the right to use. They are shown publicly on your menu.
  • Item names are written in Hindi, Kannada and Tamil by sound (for example "Masala Dosa" in that script). These are made automatically and may be wrong; you can correct them in the app.

Orders and customer payments

  • Customers pay you directly. Mark an order paid only when you have received the money.
  • When the app shows money to return (a cancelled or reduced paid order), give it back to the customer.
  • Use a customer's phone number only to contact them about their order. Never use it for marketing or share it.
  • The queue customers see is based on what you mark in the app, so keep orders up to date.

Suspension

We may pause a cart or disable an account for unpaid fees, misuse, fake orders, avoiding the platform fee, or complaints of serious harm. Where we can, we'll tell you why and how to fix it.

Our responsibility

We work to keep GullyEats running, but it may sometimes be slow or unavailable, for example when your phone or the network is offline. To the extent the law allows, GullyEats is not responsible for lost sales from an outage beyond our reasonable control, or for disputes between you and your customers.

Changes and law

We may update these terms; the date at the top shows the latest version, and using the app after a change means you accept it. These terms are governed by the laws of India, and the courts in Bengaluru, Karnataka have jurisdiction.

Platform fee

  • GullyEats charges ₹2 for each order a customer pays for, counted by the day (India time) it was paid. It is shown to customers as part of their total.
  • Each day's fee can be paid in the app through Razorpay after that day ends.
  • If a day's fee is still unpaid 26 hours after that day began (2:00 am the next day), the cart stops taking new orders until it is paid.
  • Fees for orders cancelled after their day was billed are credited on a later bill. If a fee is accidentally paid twice, the extra payment is refunded automatically. Otherwise, paid fees are not refundable.

Privacy

We collect what we need to run your cart. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

What we collect

Vendors

Name, mobile number, Vendor ID, cart name, city, address and map location, cart and menu photos, menu and prices, orders and sales, platform fee payments.

To run your account and cart, show your menu to customers, and bill the platform fee.

Staff

Name, mobile number, staff ID and the cart they work at.

To let staff log in and take orders.

Logins

PINs and one-time passwords, stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form that can't be read back.

To keep your account secure.

Fee payments

Handled by Razorpay. We keep only the payment reference, never card or UPI details.

To record which bills are paid.

Who sees it

  • Customers see your cart name, address, photos, menu and whether you're open.
  • Your staff see your cart's orders and menu, but not your sales or fee bills.
  • Companies that run GullyEats for us: Razorpay (fee payments), Amazon Web Services (photo storage), MongoDB Atlas (database), Render and Vercel (hosting), and Google Input Tools (menu item names only, to write them in Hindi, Kannada and Tamil). They handle data only to provide that service.
  • Authorities, when Indian law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account, orders and payments while your account is active and as long as tax and accounting laws require after that. Removed staff logins are deleted.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask what personal data we hold about you, and have it corrected or erased (except what the law requires us to keep). You can also nominate someone to act for you. Write to our Grievance Officer below; we reply within 90 days at the latest. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Each cart's data is visible only to its owner, its staff and GullyEats. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you as the law requires.
